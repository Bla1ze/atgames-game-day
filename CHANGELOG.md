# Changelog

## 0.9 — 20 November 2026

**Final results, top performers, and every live game at once.**

- **Final results:** when one of your teams' games ends, a banner and the DMD
  show the result, like "W 2-1 vs PHI". The score horn sounds for a win.
- **Top performers:** each game page shows both teams' leaders, like points,
  rebounds and assists.
- **Every live game at once:** when two or more of your teams play at the same
  time, the live view shows them all. The flippers choose one; A opens it.

## 0.8 — 20 November 2026

**Score alerts, a horn, and a hands-off mode.**

- **Score alerts:** when one of your teams scores, a banner slides down on any
  screen and the DMD shows GOAL!, TOUCHDOWN!, RUN! and the score. Basketball
  alerts when your team takes the lead.
- **Score horn:** an arena horn through the cabinet's speakers. Off unless you
  switch it on.
- **Hands-off mode:** leave the cabinet alone for a minute and it shows your
  live game, or each team's page in turn.
- **Settings on the cabinet:** press Start for the score horn, hands-off mode
  and the live view switches, right on the playfield.
- **Coming up:** each team page lists its next few games.
- **Live football and hockey:** a field with the ball and down and distance;
  shots on goal for hockey.
- Play times read "Q2 7:58" instead of "2nd Quarter".
- **Fixed:** the LIVE tag's text sat low.

## 0.7 — 20 November 2026

**A card for every team you follow, and a page for each one.**

- **Home:** one card per team: its live score, today's result, or its next
  game with a countdown. Today's scores is the last card.
- **Team pages:** highlight a team and press A for its game now or next (with
  the weather where it's played), its last game and its standings. The
  flippers switch teams, and the backglass and DMD follow along.
- **Today's scores:** every game today in your leagues; press A to open one.
- **Fixed:** the flippers got stuck on the Scores card.
- **Fixed:** only your first team showed up; every team you follow does now.
- **Fixed:** white team logos disappeared on highlighted cards.

## 0.6 — 20 November 2026

- The version number now shows at the bottom of the home screen, so you can
  see an update took. (This release mainly tests the in-app updater.)

## 0.5 — 20 November 2026

First public version.

- Home screen: clock, next game with countdown and channel, weather where the
  game is played, today's scores, division standings.
- Live view when a followed team plays, with a baseball diamond, count,
  pitcher and batter.
- Backglass: Team vs. Team with logos and the live score or next game.
- DMD: live score, or the time with a ticker.
- Setup from a phone: QR code and PIN; team lists and logos come through the
  cabinet.
- Times follow the cabinet's own clock.
- Everything is kept in the app's folder: data, cache, logos.
- Updates itself: a banner on the home screen offers new versions, shows
  what changed, installs and restarts. Settings are kept.
